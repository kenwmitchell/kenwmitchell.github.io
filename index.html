About Ken

I started programming sprites in BASIC on a Commodore 64 in the early 90s. Since then, I've spent the last two decades building systems and solving problems—from scripting automation in cellular networks to designing infrastructure for businesses at scale.

I believe the best technology is invisible. It works, it scales, and it lets people focus on what actually matters. Whether it's networking, infrastructure, or building products that support people's lives, I'm motivated by systems that are reliable, efficient, and thoughtfully designed.

These days, I'm focused on two ventures:

  • Yellowbox Solutions — an MSP providing end-to-end IT support for businesses
  • Resolute Care Services — supporting individuals with developmental disabilities through residential habilitation services

Both are rooted in the same philosophy: reliable, human-centered systems that make people's lives better.

Yellowbox Solutions

"Your Guiding Light for Effortless Tech Management."

We deliver seamless, end-to-end IT support that safeguards and empowers businesses. Acting as an in-house IT team for companies that aren't ready for one, we handle all aspects of network security and technical decisions so business owners can focus on what they do best.

Values: Customer Commitment, Reliability, Adaptability, Employee Support & Growth
